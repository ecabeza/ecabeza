# Hi, I'm Eric 👋

**Senior BI Developer · Data Engineer · Applied AI** — based in Puerto Rico 🇵🇷

I build governed data platforms for the public sector. As the QlikView / Qlik Sense consultant for Puerto Rico's Treasury Department (Hacienda), I design dimensional models, point-in-time reporting systems, and the enterprise BI infrastructure behind them. I hold a **Master of Information and Data Science from UC Berkeley**, and I now apply it to serverless data pipelines and LLM systems that are testable, provider-agnostic, and honest about their limits.

### What matters to me

- **Data you can defend.** Layered architectures, SCD Type 2 history, and snapshot dimensions so a number reported last year can be reproduced exactly today.
- **Work that reaches people.** I modeled federal stimulus eligibility rules against local tax-return data for a COVID-19 payment distribution effort in Puerto Rico that supported payments to more than 1.5 million people.
- **Responsible technology.** I build with privacy and subject safety in mind — from AI ethics coursework at Berkeley to face anonymization in human-rights documentation tooling.
